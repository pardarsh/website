# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

Pardarsh is about making public records open and accountable. We hold
ourselves to the same standard: be respectful, be honest, and assume good
faith.

**Expected:** empathy and kindness; respect for differing views and
experiences; giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback; taking
responsibility for mistakes.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults or personal attacks; sexualised
language or imagery; publishing others' private information without consent;
trolling or deliberately derailing discussion; any conduct that would be
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers: use the
repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability** and mark it
"Code of Conduct", or contact a maintainer directly. All reports are
reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may warn,
temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors, following the enforcement
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
